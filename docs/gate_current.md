## Other rules in force

- **Skip rule, development and validation:** a trade is not taken if a listed action's ex-date falls after its entry and on or before its exit. Source: the stored NSE corporate-action files only. Actions: split, bonus, demerger, consolidation. Daily returns on those ex-dates are empty.
- **Skip rule, forward:** only if an action of rule A's kinds, with an announced ex-date after entry and up to exit, was on the list at the signal time. an action not announced by the signal time causes no skip. The trade counts as normal, including a large gap loss. Registry: every forward signal stores the sha256 of the action list as it stood at signal time. Source: NOT DECIDED. The nightly job does not collect the list and no collection is started; manual options await the user's decision.
- **Timing:** a field public by the close of D may use D. Any other field stays at D-1. An unverified publication time counts as D-1. Baselines: baselines read D-1: prev_day, momentum_5d, the GARCH weight and the minimum-move filter. Trade entry and exit timing are unchanged. Forward uses the same.
- **Multiple testing:** the correction moves to item d: the lower bound is the (2.5 / k)th bootstrap percentile for k forward specs: 2.5th for 1, 1.25th for 2, 0.8333rd for 3. Claude's reading, for the user to confirm. Forward specs: at most 3, pre-registered.
- **Horizons:** primary [1, 2, 3]; [5, 10] are allowed, labelled "low power under item e".
- **Validation:** 5 baselines x 5 horizons x 2 universes x 20 schedules reads were logged in addendum 4; still once per frozen model spec.
- **Item g existence test:** at least 100 signal dates with at least 20 U1 stocks filled; a market feature: a value; basis_spot_index: one index.
- **Feature correction:** garch_h1, garch_h5, garch_h10: multiply the returns by 100 before garch_forecasts. Only that line changes.
- **Model suite:** if none pass, the report says no edge found at the stated trial count. No model, feature or horizon is added.
