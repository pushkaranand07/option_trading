Sources and updates

How this page is built

Prices, options value traded, put-call ratios, lot sizes and FII/Client positioning come from NSE's daily archives (F&O bhavcopy, participant-wise open interest, official index closes), verified with zero missing sessions since 2018. Regimes come from a hidden Markov model fitted only on past data. Researched events link to their official circular or news source. Research and decision support only, not investment advice.
