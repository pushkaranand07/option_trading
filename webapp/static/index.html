Every word on this page, explained

Call (CE) — an option that gains when the index goes up. Put (PE) — gains when the index goes down. You pay a premium to buy one; the most you can lose is that premium.

Lot — options trade in fixed bundles. One NIFTY lot is 65 units (NSE revises lot sizes; the app shows the current one), so a ₹100 premium costs ₹6,500 per lot.

Strike — the price level an option is tied to. We pick one strike "in the money" because it moves more closely with the index than cheap far-away strikes.

Entry — where you get in. Stop loss — the level where you exit to keep a loss small. Always place it the moment you enter. Target — where you book profit.

Intraday — buy and sell the same day; everything closes by 3:15 PM. BTST — Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow: buy near today's close, sell next morning.

VWAP — the average price traders paid today. Above it, buyers are winning the day; below it, sellers are.

EMA — a moving average. When the fast one is above the slow one, the short-term trend is up.

RSI — momentum from 0 to 100. 50–70 means healthy buying; above 70 can mean overheated.

Win rate — how often this exact setup hit its target before its stop in the past. Avg result — the average gain or loss per trade in units of risk, after costs. A high win rate with a negative average still loses money: the few losses are bigger than the many small wins.

The golden rules: never risk more than 1–2% of your money on one trade, always use a stop loss, take at most one trade a day, and skip days the page says "No trade". Not trading is a position too.

Pulse shows rule-based setups checked against recent history, and AI opinions. Past results do not guarantee future ones, and no strategy wins every time. This is not investment advice and no orders are placed. Options can lose their full premium.
