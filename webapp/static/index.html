Every word on this page, explained

Call (CE) — an option that gains when the price goes up. Put (PE) — gains when it goes down. You pay a premium to buy one; the most you can lose is that premium.

Lot — options trade in fixed bundles. One NIFTY lot is 65 units (NSE revises lot sizes; the app shows the current one), so a ₹100 premium costs ₹6,500 per lot.

Strike — the price level an option is tied to.

Entry — where you get in. Stop loss — where you exit to keep a loss small; place it the moment you enter. Target — where you book profit.

Intraday — buy and sell the same day; everything closes by 3:15 PM. BTST — Buy Today, Sell Tomorrow.

VWAP — the average price traders paid today. EMA — a moving average. RSI — momentum from 0 to 100.

Win rate — how often a setup hit its target first in the past. Avg result — the average gain or loss per trade, in units of risk, after costs. A high win rate with a negative average still loses money.

The golden rules: risk at most 1–2% of your money on one trade, always use a stop loss, at most one trade a day, and skip days that say "No trade".
